Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Sparring is published by Melvin Chongvilay, the controller of your data. Contact: sparring.support@gmail.com.

What the app does

Sparring lets you practice conversations out loud with fictional characters played by an AI. An account is optional: without one, everything stays on your phone. No advertising.

Data we process

  • Your profile. During onboarding you enter your first name, your age, your gender (man or woman) and, if you want, your last name and what you do (student, employee…), plus your goals. They are used to apply the 18+ rule and to tailor your situations. They stay on your phone unless you create an account (see below). Only your gender goes with each round: it picks the character you meet and the grammar they use with you, and it is passed to Anthropic as an instruction, without your name. The rest of your profile is never sent to the AI providers.
  • Your account (optional). If you create an account (email and password, Google or Apple), Supabase keeps your email address (with Apple, possibly a relay address) and a copy of your progress (profile, rounds, level, program) so you get it back on your other devices. Your password is stored hashed by Supabase; we never have access to it.
  • Your voice, during a round. Each line you record is sent to our server and passed to OpenAI to be transcribed into text. Sparring does not keep recordings: the file is deleted from your phone as soon as it is transcribed, and our server does not store it.
  • The text of your conversations. Transcripts are sent to Anthropic to generate the characters' replies and the end of round analysis. They are stored on your phone (your last 100 rounds) and, if you have an account, in your synced copy. Before your first round, the app asks for your explicit consent to these transfers to the AI providers; you can withdraw it in Settings (rounds are then paused).
  • Pseudonymous usage data. To prevent abuse and control costs, our server keeps, for each call: a random user identifier, the round and scenario identifiers, the type of call, your Premium status, an estimated cost and the date. This data contains neither your voice nor the text of your conversations.
  • Reports. If you report a character's message, we keep the reported message, the reason and the round identifier to improve the app's safety.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. RevenueCat tells us whether your Premium subscription is active, using an anonymous identifier. We never receive your payment details.
  • Optional analytics. They are off today. If enabled in a future version (this policy will then be updated), anonymous usage statistics (screens viewed, rounds completed) may be sent to PostHog. They never contain your conversations.
  • Reminders. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No server sends you notifications.

What we do not do

No advertising, no selling of data, no tracking across apps or websites (no "tracking" as Apple defines it), no marketing profiling.

Legal bases

Performance of the service you request (rounds, analysis, subscription, account sync), your consent to sending your voice and conversations to the AI providers, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and keeping the app secure.

Retention

  • Conversations and profile: on your phone, and in your account if you have one, until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Pseudonymous usage data: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Reports: 12 months.

Providers and transfers outside the European Union

We use Supabase (server, account and sync hosting, region European Union, in Frankfurt, Germany), Google and Apple (sign in, if you choose these options), OpenAI (transcription, character voices and moderation), Anthropic (character replies and analysis), Apple and RevenueCat (subscriptions). OpenAI, Anthropic and RevenueCat may process data in the United States, under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the Data Privacy Framework, depending on the provider.

Adults only

Sparring is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing. The simplest way: Settings > Delete my account deletes your account and your synced progress, anonymizes your usage data and wipes your phone. Without an account, Settings > Delete my data erases everything on your phone and anonymizes your data on our servers. Any subscription is cancelled in iPhone Settings. You can also write to sparring.support@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).
