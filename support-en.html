FAQ

  • Manage or cancel my subscription: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Sparring.
  • Restore purchases: in the app, Settings > Restore purchases.
  • Delete my account and data: in the app, Settings > Delete my account (or Delete my data without an account).
  • The microphone doesn't work: iPhone Settings > Sparring > Microphone. You can also type your lines.
  • Withdraw consent for the AI: in the app, Settings > Sharing with AI providers.
